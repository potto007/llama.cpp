DiffusionGemma prompt-KV store: benchmark

RTX 5090 · DiffusionGemma 26B-A4B Q4_K_M · flash-attn · ctx 32768 · seed 42. Reproduce with examples/diffusion-gemma-server/bench/diffusion_bench.py.

Four configurations across the optimization arc:

  • Baseline - pre-pad-fix (DG_FATTN_KV_PAD=1, ≈ commit 1ed05a6b7): the 6 head-dim-512 global layers fall back to CPU flash-attn.
  • F16 - pad-fixed F16 store (current default): global FA back on the GPU.
  • Q8 no-cache - SWA-layer Q8_0 store, dequantized to F32 on every denoise step.
  • Q8+cache - SWA-Q8 store + per-block dequant cache (shipped, DG_KV_STORE=q8).

4. Diffusion efficiency (config-independent)

Tokens/step ≈ 8-14 and fill ratio ≈ 1.0 (canvas full) or ~0.44 (short answers) across all configs - these are model/schedule properties. The KV-store work changed step cost, not diffusion efficiency.

The arc: the pad-256 fix took denoise from O(P) CPU-bound to flat GPU; SWA-Q8 + a per-block dequant cache then doubled the context ceiling (32k→65k) for a ~15% denoise cost and ~2.3 GB saved.

Method: the baseline column is DG_FATTN_KV_PAD=1 on the current build (isolates the pad variable; the actual 1ed05a6b7 binary crashed on the 2nd request under the current server). Its 16k / output rows are omitted because CPU-bound runs are prohibitively slow. Q8-no-cache was measured with a temporary DG_NO_DEC_CACHE toggle (not in the shipped build).
